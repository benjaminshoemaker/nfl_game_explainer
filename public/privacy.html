Privacy and analytics

Analytics is optional. Before you allow it, we do not send your visits or interactions to PostHog. You can decline and use the whole site.

If you allow analytics, we use PostHog to measure page visits, selected controls, and whether the same browser returns in later weeks. An anonymous identifier is saved in this browser’s cookies and local storage. We also record how you use this site—pages, clicks, and scrolling—to understand problems. Form inputs are masked, and console logs and network payloads are not recorded. We do not ask for your name or email.

We send page paths, selected teams or factors, device/browser information, and referring website domains. Analytics event URLs have query strings and arbitrary section anchors removed; session recordings reconstruct the pages you view. PostHog is configured to discard IP addresses. Data is processed by PostHog in its US region.

Use the Privacy button on any tracked page to change your choice. Declining stops recording and future collection and clears PostHog’s stored browser identity. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals disable analytics. Clearing browser storage or switching devices also breaks the connection between visits.

If you send feedback, the email draft includes the page URL and, when available and you have opted in, your anonymous visitor/session IDs and a private recording link. You review and send this using your email app. Recordings may be unavailable if blocked, expired, or the free recording allowance has been reached.

Our hosting provider processes requests needed to deliver the site independently of this optional analytics. See PostHog’s privacy policy and Vercel’s privacy policy.
